## Mandatory original functionality and data boundaries

Do not rewrite or delete original audio. One player and deliberate Pause win over preview and scene animation. Maintain exact current/draft revision and song/seam occurrence IDs; Undo only when actually supported. Request route intermediates require clear consent and all-or-none commit. All eight stem lanes and 8–64 cells remain operable with local recovery. Lyrics metadata disclosure, exact FLAC generation and neutral blind A/B remain as before. Authentication, cloud music, subscriptions and telemetry are excluded. Preserve all previous routes and shortcuts until their replacement is test-proven.

## Audit rule

Before an implementation task can move to Implemented, attach source/touchpoint and test evidence. Before Verified Yes, attach an independent reviewer with tested SHA, screen-reader/touch/real-audio/device records as appropriate.
