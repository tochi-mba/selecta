## Context and evidence boundary

ADR-0403 bills one person per workspace. A business pays for many venues, zones and staff seats, wants VAT-correct invoices with purchase-order numbers, and cannot accept music stopping mid-service because a card failed. Stripe models this as one subscription with several items, each with a quantity; changing a quantity can create prorations. UK B2B sales carry no consumer 14-day cancellation right, and services to EU businesses with a valid VAT number are normally zero-rated by the UK supplier with the customer reverse-charging the VAT.

This ADR records a proposed decision. Nothing in it is implemented; Stripe behaviour cited from public docs must be re-checked in test mode.

## Decision

- **One subscription per organisation** with quantity items: `business_venue` (quantity = active paid venues), `business_extra_zone` (quantity = zones above one per venue), `business_extra_seat` (seats above three per venue). Quantities are computed by the server from ADR-0442 data, never typed by a client. The ADR-0403 rule holds: every webhook is a poke, re-fetch and recompute.
- **Adding** a venue, zone or seat takes effect at once and is charged prorated (`proration_behavior=create_prorations`, collected on the next invoice; or immediately for amounts above a threshold the owner sets). **Removing** takes effect at period end with no credit by default, and the item keeps working until then.
- **Trial:** 14 days, 1 venue and 1 zone, card required; one trial per organisation and per card fingerprint.
- **B2B checkout:** the buyer confirms they act for a business; business terms (ADR-0449) are accepted and versioned; company name, billing address and tax ID are collected; the consumer cancellation flow of ADR-0403 does not apply.
- **VAT:** UK customers pay UK VAT. EU business customers with a VAT number that validates at the time of sale get a zero-rated invoice with a reverse-charge note; the validation result is stored. Without a valid number, EU sales stay blocked as in ADR-0403. Tax IDs are re-checked before each renewal.
- **Invoices:** one consolidated invoice per organisation with venue names in line descriptions and a per-venue cost breakdown; up to four custom fields (PO number at organisation level, optional per-venue cost centre). Invoice payment by bank transfer (`send_invoice`, net 30) is offered for annual plans and Enterprise.
- **Dunning without silence:** 14-day grace on a failed payment; emails to owner and admins on days 0, 3, 7 and 12; an in-app banner on manager and admin devices only, never on the customer-facing display; after grace, management becomes read-only; playback stops only at each venue's next closing time after grace, with 72 h and 24 h warnings.
- **Downgrade:** the owner picks which venues stay; unpicked venues and zones over the paid count pause at their next closing time; nothing is deleted and resuming restores them.
- **Venue changing organisation:** both organisation owners consent; the venue moves at the next billing period boundary; library rights do not move automatically.
- **Disputes:** never silence a venue automatically; a lost dispute enters the same grace path as a failed payment.
- **Enterprise:** contracts outside Stripe are stored as audited contract entitlements with term, venues, price and expiry; reconciliation leaves them alone.

## User outcome and acceptance contract

A business sees one predictable bill per month, can add a venue in a minute and see exactly what it will cost, gets invoices its accountant accepts, and never has music cut mid-service by a billing problem without days of warning.

## Edge cases

- Adding three venues in one minute: one quantity update per change, idempotent; the preview always shows the final total.
- Removing a venue then re-adding it in the same period: no second charge for the same period.
- A webhook says the subscription is `past_due` while a venue is mid-service: nothing stops; the grace clock starts.
- Grace ends at 15:00 while a venue is open until 02:00 the next day (overnight hours): it stops at 02:00, after the 72 h and 24 h warnings have been sent; a venue already past its last warning never stops earlier than its close.
- Opening hours missing or the venue set to 24 hours: stop at the first daily quiet point the owner chose, else 04:00 local, with warnings.
- The venue's internet is down at the stop time: the kiosk stops at its own known close time from its cached schedule (ADR-0446).
- VAT number fails validation at renewal: the renewal is held, the owner is told, and the grace path applies, never a silent VAT change.
- A PO number expires mid-year: the admin edits it; it applies to draft and future invoices only (finalised invoices cannot change).
- Venue transfer between organisations with different currencies: the receiving organisation's currency applies from the next period.
- Refund asked after a missed SLO: credit note against the next invoice per the service-credit policy (ADR-0448), not a card refund by default.
- A chargeback on an annual payment: billing staff review; the venue keeps playing during the dispute.
- Enterprise contract expiry with no renewal: 30, 14 and 3 day warnings to the owner and REX Technologies sales, then the grace path.

## Rejected alternatives

- One Stripe subscription per venue (many invoices, harder volume discounts, more failure points).
- Stopping playback immediately on failure (a silent venue is the worst failure and loses the customer).
- Crediting removed venues mid-period (encourages churn games and complicates invoices).

## Implementation checklist - 13/15 Implemented, 1/15 In progress, 1/15 Not started; 0/15 independently Verified

1. **BZ-0447-01 - Business prices and catalogue map.** **Acceptance:** Stripe products and prices for venue, extra zone and extra seat, monthly and annual, per currency, created by a test-mode setup script and mapped server-side to `business_venue_v1`; flag `business.billing` registered (default off). **Required verification:** Unit tests with a fake Stripe. **Implementation:** Implemented (`selecta business-stripe-setup` (flag `business.billing`) creates the products, prices and volume coupon in Stripe test mode, refuses live keys, and prints the price map for `SELECTA_BUSINESS_STRIPE_PRICES`; flag `business.billing`; tests in `tests/test_bz_billing_plan.py`). **Independently Verified:** No.
2. **BZ-0447-02 - Quantity subscription and reconciliation.** **Acceptance:** One subscription per organisation; quantities computed from venues, zones and seats; reconciliation finds and fixes quantity drift with an audit entry, and a second run finds nothing. **Required verification:** Integration tests with a fake Stripe. **Implementation:** Implemented (`billing.reconcile` runs in the `business.billing` maintenance pass, `billing_jobs.run_pass`; tests in `tests/test_bz_billing_routes.py`). **Independently Verified:** No.
3. **BZ-0447-03 - Add and remove mid-period with proration.** **Acceptance:** Adding a venue, zone or seat is immediate and prorated, with a cost preview from Stripe's upcoming invoice before confirming; removing takes effect at period end; remove then re-add in one period never double-charges. **Required verification:** Integration tests with a fake Stripe and a time-travel clock. **Implementation:** Implemented (a venue, zone or seat added is charged at once and prorated by the request hook in `billing_plan.install_change_hook`; removals take effect at period end; the period high-water mark stops a second charge; the cost preview comes from Stripe's upcoming invoice; tested with a fake Stripe, including remove then re-add in one period; no time-travel test across a renewal yet). **Independently Verified:** No.
4. **BZ-0447-04 - Trial.** **Acceptance:** 14-day trial with 1 venue and 1 zone and a card; one trial per organisation and per card fingerprint; conversion or lapse behaves as specified. **Required verification:** Integration tests. **Implementation:** Implemented (trial chosen at checkout, created by `billing.start_trial` from the card collected on Stripe Checkout; `tests/test_bz_billing_checkout.py`). **Independently Verified:** No.
5. **BZ-0447-05 - Volume discount and Enterprise threshold.** **Acceptance:** 10 to 24 venues bill at 10% off; 25 or more cannot check out self-serve and are routed to a sales form. **Required verification:** Unit and integration tests. **Implementation:** Implemented (volume coupon set by `ensure_subscription` and reconciliation; 25 or more venues refused with `enterprise_required` and shown as Enterprise on the billing page; the coupon must exist in Stripe test mode). **Independently Verified:** No.
6. **BZ-0447-06 - B2B checkout.** **Acceptance:** Checkout captures the business confirmation, business terms version, company name, billing address and tax ID; the consumer cancellation acknowledgement is not shown for business plans. **Required verification:** Playwright test against a fake Stripe. **Implementation:** Implemented (`POST /api/business/billing/checkout` records the business confirmation, terms version, company, address and VAT number, then Stripe Checkout in setup mode collects the card; completion by return or by the signed webhook; vitest in `web/src/business/console/CheckoutCard.test.tsx`; no Playwright test yet). **Independently Verified:** No.
7. **BZ-0447-07 - B2B VAT and reverse charge.** **Acceptance:** UK customers are charged UK VAT; an EU business with a VAT number valid at the time of sale gets a zero-rated invoice with a reverse-charge note; the validation result and time are stored; tax IDs are re-checked before renewal; invalid numbers keep EU sales blocked. **Required verification:** Integration tests with a fake Stripe Tax and a fake VIES response. **Implementation:** Implemented (VAT numbers checked with VIES at checkout, result and time stored; EU sales without a valid number blocked; renewal recheck in the maintenance pass holds the renewal and starts grace). **Independently Verified:** No.
8. **BZ-0447-08 - Invoices with PO numbers and venue breakdown.** **Acceptance:** Organisation PO number and per-venue cost centre appear as invoice custom fields; line descriptions name venues; a per-venue cost breakdown CSV downloads from the billing page; bank-transfer invoices with net 30 are available for annual plans. **Required verification:** Integration tests plus a Playwright test for the download. **Implementation:** Implemented (PO number and cost centres as invoice custom fields, venue names on draft invoice lines from the `invoice.created` webhook, cost breakdown CSV download on the billing page, bank transfer net 30 for yearly plans; no Playwright download test yet). **Independently Verified:** No.
9. **BZ-0447-09 - Dunning without silence.** **Acceptance:** A failed payment starts 14-day grace; emails go to owner and admins on days 0, 3, 7 and 12; the banner shows on manager devices only and never on the customer display; after grace, management is read-only; playback stops only at each venue's next closing time with 72 h and 24 h warnings, including overnight hours and missing hours. **Required verification:** Integration tests with a time-travel clock plus a Playwright test that the customer display never shows billing state. **Implementation:** Implemented (14-day grace, emails, manager-only banner, read-only, 72 h and 24 h warnings and the stop at close in `billing_jobs.run_pass`; a stopped venue is refused when it next starts playing; no Playwright customer-display test yet). **Independently Verified:** No.
10. **BZ-0447-10 - Downgrade chooses venues.** **Acceptance:** Reducing venues asks the owner which stay; unpicked venues and zones over the paid count pause at their next close; nothing is deleted; resuming restores schedules, crates and devices. **Required verification:** Integration tests plus a Playwright test of the picker. **Implementation:** Implemented (`POST /api/business/billing/downgrade` keeps the chosen venues; the others, and zones over a lowered paid count, pause at their next close in the maintenance pass; resume restores them; the picker is on the billing page; vitest, no Playwright test yet). **Independently Verified:** No.
11. **BZ-0447-11 - Venue transfer between organisations.** **Acceptance:** Both owners consent; the venue, its zones, devices, licence attestations and play logs move at the next period boundary; library tracks stay with the source organisation unless the receiving owner re-uploads or attests rights; both subscriptions' quantities update; every step is audited. **Required verification:** Integration tests. **Implementation:** Implemented (`billing_transfer`: both owners consent through the billing page, the venue with its zones, players, licences and play logs moves at the source's period end, library songs, crates and programmes stay, both sides audited; quantities reconcile afterwards). **Independently Verified:** No.
12. **BZ-0447-12 - Refunds, disputes and chargebacks.** **Acceptance:** Refunds and service credits are staff actions with reasons; a dispute never stops a venue; a lost dispute enters the grace path; the written B2B refund policy is linked from the business terms. **Required verification:** Integration tests plus doc review. **Implementation:** In progress (staff credits and refunds with a reason behind `business.billing.refund`; disputes never stop a venue and a lost one enters grace, via the signed webhook. **Owner item:** the written B2B refund policy is legal wording the owner must supply and link from the business terms; it is not written here). **Independently Verified:** No.
13. **BZ-0447-13 - Enterprise contracts outside Stripe.** **Acceptance:** Staff record a contract (term, venues, zones, seats, price, invoice terms, expiry) as an audited entitlement source that never pretends Stripe charged; reconciliation skips it; expiry warnings at 30, 14 and 3 days. **Required verification:** Integration tests. **Implementation:** Implemented (staff record and revoke contracts at `POST /api/admin/business/orgs/{org_id}/contracts` with `business.billing.contracts`; reconciliation and immediate charges skip a live contract; 30, 14 and 3 day warnings to owners, admins and REX Technologies sales, then the grace path; contract and dunning emails go out through the `emails` task). **Independently Verified:** No.
