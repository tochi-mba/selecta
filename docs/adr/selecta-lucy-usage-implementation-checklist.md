# Selecta Lucy Usage implementation checklist (ADR-0480 to ADR-0487)

**Task status (recounted from the task lines by `src/selecta/audit/checklist_summary.py`):** 52 tasks: 4 Implemented, 41 In progress, 7 Not started, 0 Needs a person, 0 Superseded. 6 name a person-only step. 4 ticked. 0 independently Verified.

**Status:** Eight ADRs written on 2026-11-25 against `main` at `f5b6a4a` and accepted by the owner the same day. They build on ADR-0402, ADR-0404, ADR-0464 and ADR-0476 without changing their statuses. Building is in progress behind flags that are off for everyone; a line below is ticked only when its evidence exists. Every size (window, share, multiplier, pack price) is a hypothesis until LM-0487-02 and LM-0487-03 are done.

**How to tick a box.** Tick `[x]` only after the acceptance statement is true and the required verification has run. The line must then name implementation evidence after `Evidence:` (a test, a commit or a CI run). A test with a faked clock or a faked provider proves the logic, not the staging behaviour; staging evidence is required wherever stated. Independent verification is a separate field and is changed only by someone other than the builder.

**Order.** LM-0480 and LM-0487-01 first (flag and shadow ledger), then LM-0481, LM-0482 and LM-0484 in shadow, then measurement (LM-0487-02, -03), then LM-0483 and LM-0485, then rollout. LM-0486 waits for the owner.
