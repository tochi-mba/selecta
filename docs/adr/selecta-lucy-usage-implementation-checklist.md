# Selecta Lucy Usage implementation checklist (ADR-0480 to ADR-0487)

**Task status (recounted from the task lines by `src/selecta/audit/checklist_summary.py`):** 52 tasks: 1 Implemented, 0 In progress, 51 Not started, 0 Needs a person, 0 Superseded. 6 name a person-only step. 1 ticked. 0 independently Verified.

**Status:** Eight proposed ADRs written on 2026-11-25 against `main` at `f5b6a4a`, as a docs-only set. They build on ADR-0402, ADR-0404, ADR-0464 and ADR-0476 without changing their statuses. Nothing here is built. Every size (window, share, multiplier, pack price) is a hypothesis until LM-0487-02 and LM-0487-03 are done.

**How to tick a box.** Tick `[x]` only after the acceptance statement is true and the required verification has run. The line must then name implementation evidence after `Evidence:` (a test, a commit or a CI run). A test with a faked clock or a faked provider proves the logic, not the staging behaviour; staging evidence is required wherever stated. Independent verification is a separate field and is changed only by someone other than the builder.

**Order.** LM-0480 and LM-0487-01 first (flag and shadow ledger), then LM-0481, LM-0482 and LM-0484 in shadow, then measurement (LM-0487-02, -03), then LM-0483 and LM-0485, then rollout. LM-0486 waits for the owner.

## [ADR-0481 - One cost-weighted Lucy allowance](adr-0481-one-cost-weighted-lucy-allowance.md)

- [ ] **LM-0481-01 - Unit and ledger.** Acceptance: a tenant-isolated allowance ledger records, per turn, the units reserved and the units charged, where one unit is one millionth of a US dollar at the pinned deployment's prices; migrations are reversible. Required verification: schema, isolation and migration tests. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0481-02 - Reserve at admission, charge at settlement.** Acceptance: admission reserves the tier's per-turn cap atomically and settlement replaces it with real cost; two sends racing for the last of an allowance admit exactly one. Required verification: concurrency tests on Postgres, including two devices. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0481-03 - Charging rules.** Acceptance: completed, stopped, failed, timed-out and retried-down-the-chain turns are charged exactly as ADR-0481 rule 5 states, and platform spend still books every dollar. Required verification: one test per outcome asserting both the allowance and the platform ledger. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0481-04 - Class no longer charges.** Acceptance: no admission or charge reads `RequestClass`; changing the classifier's answer for a message changes neither whether it is admitted nor what it costs the person. Required verification: property test over classifier outputs. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0481-05 - One record for every reader.** Acceptance: admission, the meter, the admin economics view and Download my data read the same ledger; no second counter exists for display. Required verification: a test that fails if any Lucy usage figure is computed outside the ledger module. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0481-06 - Overshoot bounded.** Acceptance: a window can end at most one per-turn cap over its limit, and the excess counts toward the week. Required verification: boundary tests at each tier's cap. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.

## [ADR-0482 - Rolling session window and weekly limit](adr-0482-rolling-session-window-and-weekly-limit.md)

- [ ] **LM-0482-03 - Both windows gate admission.** Acceptance: a turn is admitted only with room in both; the refusal records which one was spent. Required verification: tests for session spent, week spent and both. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0482-04 - Plan changes.** Acceptance: an upgrade raises both limits at once and keeps what was used; a downgrade applies at the next weekly reset. Required verification: tests with the Stripe test clock. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0482-05 - Server clock only.** Acceptance: no window calculation reads a device clock; a device hours fast or slow sees the same reset times. Required verification: Playwright test with a skewed browser clock. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0482-06 - Venue pools.** Acceptance: a business venue's pool is held in units with a weekly limit and no session window, and is still checked before a personal allowance. Required verification: business entitlement tests and the business Playwright suite. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.

## [ADR-0484 - One truthful Lucy usage meter](adr-0484-one-truthful-lucy-usage-meter.md)

- [ ] **LM-0484-01 - One read model.** Acceptance: a single function returns session and week share, reset times, state and whether a lighter Lucy is answering, from the allowance ledger; Lucy's settings, Plan and billing, the stream, the prompt and the admin view all call it. Required verification: unit tests, and a test that the figure shown equals the figure enforced after any sequence of turns. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0484-02 - Meter on the page.** Acceptance: "This session" and "This week" each show a bar, the percentage left and a reset time in plain words; a session not yet opened reads as full; no tokens, dollars, units, model names or turn counts appear. Required verification: Playwright in light and dark themes at phone and desktop widths. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0484-03 - Rounding and states.** Acceptance: 100% left only when nothing is used, 0% left only when spent, otherwise 1% to 99% left; `near` from 80% used. Required verification: table-driven unit tests at each boundary. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0484-04 - Live on every device.** Acceptance: a `lucy.usage` stream event updates the meter on every signed-in device when a turn is admitted, settles or a window resets. Required verification: two-device Playwright test. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0484-05 - Labelled estimate.** Acceptance: "about N more messages like your recent ones" appears only on the settings page, only after 5 settled turns, always with "about", from the person's own median. Required verification: unit tests and a copy check. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0484-06 - Lucy answers about her own limits.** Acceptance: asked how much is left, Lucy gives the same state and times as the page and never a number of units or turns. Required verification: evaluation cases added to `tests/lucy_eval/`. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0484-07 - Accessible meter.** Acceptance: each bar has a text equivalent, state is not carried by colour alone, and a change of state is announced once. Required verification: axe run plus a manual screen reader pass. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
- [ ] **LM-0484-08 - Usage ring.** Acceptance: a compact ring in Lucy's side rail shows the share left of the window closer to running out; hover, focus or tap shows "5-hour: N% left" and "Weekly: N% left" with reset times and a link to the full meter; states `near` and `spent` are marked by more than colour; it is drawn from SelectaDJ's own tokens and icons, respects reduced motion, and is absent with the flag off. Required verification: Playwright in light and dark themes at phone and desktop widths with mouse, keyboard and touch; axe run; a visual baseline. Implementation: Not started. Independently verified: No.
