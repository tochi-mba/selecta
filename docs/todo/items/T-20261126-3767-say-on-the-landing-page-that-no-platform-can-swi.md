Pacemaker, Serato Pyro, Jukestar and djay's Spotify mode all stopped working when Spotify changed its rules. SelectaDJ mixes files the member owns, read on their own device. A short line such as 'Your files. No platform can switch them off.' plus what SelectaDJ keeps (titles, a fingerprint, measurements) answers the fear of lock-in that DJs say keeps them off rented software. Say only what the privacy notice already promises.
