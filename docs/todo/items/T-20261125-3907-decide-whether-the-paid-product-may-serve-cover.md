ADR-0478 fetches missing covers from the Cover Art Archive and stores them. Its own page grants no licence: Use the images at your own risk; covers belong to their owners. What to decide, ideally with counsel: serve them in the paid product (with the admin hide tool as the takedown path), or keep web covers off and show only art from the person's own files. The flag library.art_lookup stays off for customers until decided.
