Cases reasoned out, not yet checked against the code. For each: read the code, say whether it is handled, and fix or file what is not. MONEY: (1) playback streams lossless audio at about 40 MB a song, about 700 MB for an hour's mix on a phone plan, with no compressed stream; (2) playback files are about six times the upload and a 45-minute export is about 530 MB: do they count against the customer's storage, and are old exports and failed attempts' files ever deleted; (3) is there a cap on one account uploading thousands of songs or pressing Make another mix repeatedly; (4) an analysis or render version bump invalidates every stored result for every customer at once; (5) what happens to uploaded music and running jobs when a subscription is cancelled, downgraded or past due. SAFETY AND LEGAL: (6) hosted originals and exported mixes need a takedown path; (7) account deletion removes originals, playback files, transitions, exports and worker scratch, and signed links issued before sign-out stop mattering; (8) if staff switch hosted storage off for an account the customer can still get their music back; (9) hostile or odd files reaching ffmpeg on a worker: crafted files, a ten-hour mixtape, a header that lies about length, unusual sample rates and channel counts. CORRECTNESS: (10) the export has no limiter, so a quiet song given positive gain could clip; (11) music with no steady beat: live, classical, ambient, tempo changes; (12) a one-song library, 500 songs (planning reads at most 60), a 20-second clip, a one-hour track; (13) the duplicate rule merges clean and explicit or live and studio versions that share a title and artist within 3 seconds; (14) a song deleted or replaced while it is in a mix that is rendering, playing or exporting. DEVICES: (15) a locked phone screen throttles the timers the player schedules audio on; calls and Bluetooth changes interrupt it; (16) remote controls, guest requests in rooms and Lucy's Save and play against the hosted player; (17) the same song both uploaded and on the device: which plays, and is it listed twice; (18) two devices pressing Make a mix or Export at once. OPERATIONS: (19) alerts only show inside the console: nothing emails or texts the owner when the gateway or workers are down; (20) workers and gateway on different versions during a deploy; (21) one customer's bulk upload queueing ahead of someone waiting to press Play; (22) Modal or the model host being down. PRODUCT: (23) hosted customers get worse transitions than free browser users until stems are allowed (T-20261125-c77c).
