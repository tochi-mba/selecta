Spotify Premium is £12.99 a month in the UK since November 2025, exactly SelectaDJ Premium's price, so visitors will compare a DJ for their own files with all the music in the world. Options: keep £12.99 and say plainly what it replaces (a hired DJ for one night, a request app, planning a set by hand) and anchor it against the party budget rather than other apps; or price Premium under £10. Pricing too low also signals a toy (price as a quality signal). The owner decides; ideally after the willingness-to-pay talks in the beta item.
