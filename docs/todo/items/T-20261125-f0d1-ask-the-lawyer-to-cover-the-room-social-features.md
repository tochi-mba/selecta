ADR-0488 adds five guest features to party rooms and venue rooms: a vote between offered songs, a heart kept on the phone, love and blend taps shown as counts, a dedication picked from a fixed list, and an end-of-night totals card. None takes typed text, photos or the camera. The Rooms legal reviews are still open (SC-0416-07 and BZ-0451-11). When the lawyer looks at Rooms, ask for a view on these five as well: whether a fixed-list dedication shown with a generated nickname, and reaction counts, change the Online Safety Act position, and whether the guest privacy notice needs more than naming votes and reactions.
