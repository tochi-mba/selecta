Every size in the Lucy Usage set is a starting guess: a weekly limit of the present monthly ceiling times 12 over 52, a session of one quarter of the week, Standard at 14 times Free and Premium at 26 times. Blocked until four weeks of shadow measurement exist. What you need to do then: read the sizing report and choose the figures so that a member at the limit costs at most 40% of the plan's net price, about 1 paid member in 20 reaches the weekly limit, and nobody gets less than today. Also decide then whether to sell extra usage packs at all (LM-0486-01).
