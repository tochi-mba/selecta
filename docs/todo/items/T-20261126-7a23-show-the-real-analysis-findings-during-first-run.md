Showing the work raises how much people value a result (labor illusion, Buell and Norton 2011; operational transparency, Buell, Kim and Tsay 2017), most of all when quality is hard to judge, as with music analysis. Today first-run progress shows counts (ADR-0141) and the reason for each blend is folded away in a details element (web/src/views/Now.tsx, WhyThisTransition, flag listen.why_transition).

Good looks like: while the library is analysed, real findings arrive as they are measured ('124 BPM, key 8A', 'lyric link: midnight to Midnight City'). On the first few blends a member hears, Why this transition is open by default, then folds away. Never add a delay to look busy, never show retries as progress, and never slow anything during a live room. Needs a flag.
