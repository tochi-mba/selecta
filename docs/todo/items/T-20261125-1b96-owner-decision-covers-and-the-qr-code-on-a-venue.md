Decisions needed, from the edge-case audit: (1) Should catalogue covers show large on the room display and guest phones at all for venues, given a wrong match or an explicit sleeve would be shown to a room? Option: a venue setting 'Show album covers on the display', off by default for business accounts. (2) The room QR code and the Stage wash are static for hours; on OLED and plasma TVs that risks burn-in. Accept, or add a slow few-pixel drift of the QR and a dimmed idle state? (3) Some TVs overscan 3 to 5 percent; should the display keep a safe margin around the QR and room code? Check on the venue TV: open the room display full screen and confirm the QR and code are fully visible.
